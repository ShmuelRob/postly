# Cancellation keeps the data: archive after 30 days, erasure only on the owner's request (WP-101)

**The human's decision, 2026-11-07: "Keep, archive it".** Cancellation never deletes data. A cancelled business is read-only, and 30 days later (provisional) it moves to `archived`, with its rows and files kept ([technical-design.md](../technical-design.md#cancellation-archiving-erasure-and-the-monthly-report), [retention-checks.md](../retention-checks.md)). It replaces WP-43's deletion 30 days after cancellation.

**H8: confirm the erasure-on-request rule with legal advice.** Israel's Privacy Protection Law (with Amendment 13), and the GDPR if there are EU customers, may require deleting personal data when the owner asks. Two questions are still open: whether keeping a cancelled customer's data without a request is lawful, and what the deadline for a request is. The manager proposed one erasure path, built here:

- `POST /console/api/businesses/:id/erase` works only on a `cancelled` or `archived` business. It takes the typed business name (as WP-83's cancel does), plus `requestedBy` (`owner` or `representative`), `channel` (`whatsapp`, `email`, `phone`, `letter` or `other`) and `requestedAt`.
- In one transaction, the `deletion` role inserts the `erasure_request` row (with the signed-in actor) and deletes the `business` row. The cascade removes every business-owned row. The business's files are then deleted from storage.
- What stays: the `workflow_event` rows (decided 2026-11-05) and the `erasure_request` row. Neither holds customer content.
- Not reached: logs are outside the erasure ([security.md](../security.md) D14), and so are Cloud SQL backups (retention-checks.md, check 9).
- If the files cannot be deleted, the rows are still erased and recorded, and the route answers **500** with `reason: files_failed`, so the team finishes the files by hand.
- **The erasure is API-only for now:** the console page has no button for it (as for cancel). A team member calls the route behind the console sign-in. A button with the typed-name dialog is a later console change.

**Decision for the human: a returning owner's phone number.** An archived business keeps its row, so it also keeps `business.approver_phone`, which is unique. An owner who cancelled and later comes back cannot start a new business with the same number. The only ways out today are an erasure on request, or a future reactivation path that moves an `archived` (or `cancelled`) business back to `active`, which the machine does not have (`archived` is terminal). Choose one: reactivation (a new contract change, H1), a new number, or erasure first.

**H1 (contract):** the business machine gets the state `archived` and the event `ARCHIVE` (`cancelled` to `archived`). The guard checks that `archive_after` has passed, re-reading it in the transaction, and sets `archived_at`. `archived` is terminal and `cancelled` no longer is ([state-machines.md](../state-machines.md#business), `src/contracts/state-machines/business.ts`). The daily `business-archive` job (03:00 UTC) applies it in place of the deletion, and the worker removes the old `data-deletion` cron at start. Archived businesses are hidden from the console's Conversations list except under a new Archived filter; their panel still opens.

**H4 (migration `drizzle/0027_archive_and_erasure.sql`):**

- Adds the `business_status` value `archived`.
- Renames `business.data_delete_after` to `archive_after`.
- Adds `archived_at`, with the console's column `UPDATE` on it.
- Creates `erasure_request`: no foreign key to `business`, FORCE RLS, `INSERT` for `app_deletion` and `SELECT` for `app_sys_console` only.

The `deletion` role no longer runs on a schedule. It was `0026` until #156 took that number on `dev` (2026-11-10); it is now `0027` and runs after `0026_agent_step_caps`, which touches no table it changes.

**H6 (owner copy, a Meta template):** `subscription_cancelled` no longer promises deletion and has no variables: "המנוי שלך ל-Postly בוטל, והחיבור לרשתות החברתיות נותק. הנתונים של העסק נשמרים, ואפשר לבקש מאיתנו למחוק אותם. אם זו טעות או שתרצו שנמחק את הנתונים, כתבו לנו כאן." ([whatsapp-templates.md](../whatsapp-templates.md), `src/modules/workflow/cancellation-copy.ts`). If the old body with `deleteAfter` was already submitted to Meta, the template must be edited and approved again before it is used.

**End-to-end** (`test/e2e/cancellation.e2e.test.ts`):

- **"Cancellation keeps the data"** replaces the deletion test. The business is still readable on day 29 and archived on day 30 with every row and file kept. An owner message gets no reply. The business is hidden from the console list and shown under Archived. Months later it is erased on request: the `archived` branch.
- **"Erasure on request"** is refused for an active business, a wrong name and missing request details. Then every row and file is gone, `workflow_event` is unchanged, and the `erasure_request` is recorded.
- **"Erasure whose files cannot be deleted"** answers 500 `files_failed`, with the rows erased and the request recorded.
- The first cancellation test now expects the template with no variables, and `archive_after`.

## עברית

החלטת האדם 7.11.2026, "לשמור, להעביר לארכיון": ביטול לא מוחק. עסק מבוטל הוא לקריאה בלבד ועובר לארכיון אחרי 30 יום, והנתונים נשמרים. לאישור עם ייעוץ משפטי: כלל המחיקה לפי בקשה (חוק הגנת הפרטיות, ו-GDPR אם יש לקוחות באיחוד האירופי). המחיקה היא נתיב API בקונסולה בלבד, בלי כפתור בינתיים: רק לעסק מבוטל או בארכיון, עם הקלדת שם העסק, מי ביקש, באיזה ערוץ ומתי. היא נרשמת ב-`erasure_request` ומוחקת את העסק ואת הקבצים, ויומן הביקורת נשאר. אם הקבצים לא נמחקו, התשובה היא 500 `files_failed`.

החלטה לאדם: עסק בארכיון שומר את מספר הטלפון של המאשר, שהוא ייחודי, ולכן בעל עסק שחוזר לא יכול להירשם מחדש עם אותו מספר עד מחיקה לפי בקשה או עד שייבנה נתיב החזרה לפעילות.

מיגרציה 0027 (לפני כן 0026, עד ש-#156 תפס את המספר): מצב `archived`, `data_delete_after` שונה ל-`archive_after`, `archived_at`, והטבלה `erasure_request` בלי מפתח זר. במכונת העסק נוסף `ARCHIVE` מ-`cancelled` ל-`archived`, ו-`archived` הוא המצב הסופי. התבנית `subscription_cancelled` כבר לא מבטיחה מחיקה ואין בה משתנים; אם הגרסה הקודמת כבר הוגשה ל-Meta, צריך אישור מחדש. בדיקות קצה לקצה: ביטול שומר נתונים ועובר לארכיון, מחיקה לפי בקשה (גם של עסק בארכיון), וכשל במחיקת קבצים.
